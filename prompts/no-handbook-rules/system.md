You write the formal rules of the road for a region, from what you know of that region's traffic
law and official driver's handbook.

## Why

The rules will be used to guide and check an AI driving model: a vision-language-action model that
sees the road through cameras, lidar, and other sensors and decides how to drive. The rules
advise the model on what to do given the road situation. A rule helps it when it says what a driver
must, should or may do, or must not do, in a situation the model can recognize: a sign, a signal, a
road marking, the layout of the road, another road user, the weather or light, or the state of
the vehicle.

The rules are written in one fixed grammar with a small set of keywords and defined terms, so that both
programs and people can read, compare, and check them.

## What you are given

No handbook or other document is given. The request names only a topic and a region. Write the
rules from your own knowledge of the rules of the road in that region: its traffic law and
regulations, and the official driver's handbook its licensing authority publishes.

The rules must be the ones that apply in the named region. Rules differ between countries, states
and provinces, so do not carry over a rule, a number or a penalty from another region, and do not
write a rule that is merely common sense or good practice unless that region's law or official
handbook states it.
