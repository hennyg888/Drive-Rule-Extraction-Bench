Passing (overtaking): a driver moving past another vehicle going the same way, whether that vehicle is travelling, stopped in traffic or parked, and everything that decides whether, where, when and how a driver may do so, including lane use for passing, duties that make passing a stopped vehicle illegal (such as having to stop for it), and what a driver who is being passed should do. "Pass" used only to mean going by a person, an animal, a work zone or another hazard is not this topic.
